### 👨‍🔬 Profile & Research Interests

I am a **Machine Learning Engineer** with a comprehensive **Full-Stack development** background, now actively seeking a PhD position for Fall [Year] or Spring [Year].

My professional experience building end-to-end systems has given me a deep appreciation for architecture, optimization, and user-centric design. However, it has also exposed me to the fundamental challenges and limitations of current AI systems. This has ignited my passion to move from application to innovation.

I am driven to pursue doctoral research to contribute to the next generation of intelligent systems. My primary research interests lie at the intersection of:

*   🤖 **Agentic AI & Automation:** Designing and building autonomous agents that can reason, plan, and execute complex, multi-step tasks.
*   🧠 **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Exploring model architectures, fine-tuning methodologies, and alignment techniques to create more capable and reliable models.
*   📚 **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** Developing advanced techniques to ground LLMs in external, verifiable knowledge, enhancing their factuality and reducing hallucination.
*   💬 **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Investigating core challenges in language understanding, reasoning, and generation.

My goal is to develop novel frameworks and models that make AI agents more dependable, scalable, and adaptable to real-world complexities.

*   🔭 I'm currently exploring state-of-the-art implementations of autonomous AI agents.
*   🌱 I am deepening my knowledge of causal inference and reinforcement learning.
*   💬 I am open to discussing PhD opportunities and research collaborations.
